[summary]
For ENES100 at the University of Maryland, our team of eight is designing and building an over-terrain vehicle (OTV) that navigates an arena autonomously — including climbing a log obstacle — and completes a fire-suppression mission, all within a $320 budget.

I work on the hardware and the mission subsystem. The robot runs on an Arduino Mega with four mecanum wheels driven by 12 V gearmotors. For the mission, a 5-channel flame sensor on a 3D-printed pedestal locates the candles, and blower fans with custom 3D-printed nozzles direct air to put them out. Two HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensors handle obstacle detection, and an ArUco marker lets the arena's vision system track our position.
