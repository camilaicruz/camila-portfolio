[summary]
A flight-stick grip on a low wedge base that works as a wireless Bluetooth mouse. A spring-centered 2-axis potentiometer joystick in the base reads stick tilt for precise cursor control, while an MPU-6050 inside the grip turns a twist of the handle into scrolling. The same motion sensor wakes the board from deep sleep when someone touches the stick, saving battery.

The base has an OLED status screen (battery, speed setting, connection state), three arcade buttons for drag-lock, speed and mode, and two thumb buttons on the grip for clicks. It runs on a Seeed XIAO ESP32-S3 with a built-in LiPo charger, inside a 3D-printed shell with a laser-cut acrylic top plate.

It started as a handheld tilt "air mouse" and grew into a flight-stick so it could also serve a real accessibility use case: precise cursor control without gripping or sliding a regular mouse.
