About me

Engineering that's built for people.

I'm an Electrical Engineering student at the University of Maryland with a passion for bridging low-level hardware design and user-centered innovation. My work spans embedded systems, circuit integration, and 3D modeling — focused on creating functional, ergonomic tech.

I was born and raised in San Juan, Puerto Rico, where I graduated from CeDIn High School with First Honors all four years. I spent those years leading student initiatives as National Honor Society President, serving as Treasurer of our Surfrider Foundation chapter, and organizing community drives — including one that collected over $1,000 in care items for pediatric cancer patients. That built a strong foundation in project organization, public speaking, and visual design.

Now I combine those skills with technical problem-solving and rapid prototyping: building a wireless BLE flight-stick mouse on my own, and a fire-suppression over-terrain vehicle with my ENES100 team. Before college I also designed an ergonomic earphone in SolidWorks at Georgia Tech's College of Design summer program, which is where I fell for human-centered design.

When I'm not working on circuits or layouts, I'm at the beach, reviewing movies, collecting shoes, or catching a live concert back home in the island sun.
